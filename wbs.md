# Work Breakdown Structure (WBS)

This file is the human-readable source of truth for renovation work.

You can edit this file manually at any time. Any scripts/tools must preserve
the structure and keep it easy to read.

---

# 1. Pre-Construction & Planning

Pre-construction covers paperwork, utilities, coordination and physical preparation
before any destructive or noisy work starts. The goal is to avoid surprises, fines
and damage to existing elements.

## 1.1 Administrative & Permissions

- 1.1.1 Verify building rules and renovation time windows
	- Check allowed working hours, noisy work limits, elevator/entrance usage rules.
- 1.1.2 Obtain renovation approval from building management / HOA (if required)
	- Submit layout sketches, scope description, contractor info.
- 1.1.3 Check if any official permits are required
	- Structural changes, moving wet areas, major electrical upgrades, gas changes.
- 1.1.4 Notify neighbors if necessary
	- Inform about noisy periods, duration, and contact person.

## 1.2 Utilities: Electricity

- 1.2.1 Apply for new electricity connection / contract (if needed)
	- Choose tariff, capacity (amperage), single vs three-phase as available.
- 1.2.2 Request capacity increase / new meter (if needed)
	- Submit application to utility provider or via building management.
- 1.2.3 Verify and document existing electrical installation
	- Photos of panel, existing circuits, visible defects, unsafe elements.
- 1.2.4 Plan temporary power supply for construction
	- Extension cords, distribution boxes, protection (RCD/RCBO) for tools.

## 1.3 Utilities: Gas (if present)

- 1.3.1 Verify existing gas installation and allowed modifications
	- Get building/utility rules on moving appliances, closing/opening points.
- 1.3.2 Apply for gas system modification / inspection
	- Request official contractor or certified specialist as required by local code.
- 1.3.3 Decide future gas usage
	- Keep gas stove vs switch to electric, close unused lines, plan ventilation.
- 1.3.4 Schedule gas shutdown / reconnection windows
	- Coordinate with other trades and apartment usage.

## 1.4 Area Survey & Documentation

- 1.4.1 Measure all rooms, openings and heights
	- Create simple dimensioned sketches (paper or digital).
- 1.4.2 Document existing finishes and conditions
	- Walls, floors, ceilings, windows, radiators, visible defects (cracks, damp).
- 1.4.3 Locate and mark existing utilities
	- Electrical outlets, switches, water pipes, drains, gas lines, radiators.
- 1.4.4 Take full photo/video walk-through of the flat
	- Before any work starts, for reference and disputes.

## 1.5 Protection & Area Preparation

- 1.5.1 Plan protected zones (items that must not be damaged)
	- Windows, doors to keep, radiators, existing floors if staying.
- 1.5.2 Install temporary protection
	- Cover floors with protective sheets/boards, protect corners and door frames.
- 1.5.3 Protect common areas in building
	- Cover elevator walls/floor, stair landings and corridors along the route.
- 1.5.4 Plan waste removal and storage
	- Decide where rubble bags are stored, container location, route for removal.

## 1.6 Logistics & Site Setup

- 1.6.1 Define access routes for workers and materials
	- Which entrance, which elevator, where keys are stored, who opens.
- 1.6.2 Decide material delivery and storage areas
	- Inside flat and/or separate storage space; max weight limits on floors.
- 1.6.3 Arrange temporary construction utilities & facilities
	- Temporary site electricity (distribution board, protected outlets, cable routing).
	- Temporary toilet for workers (or agreed shared facility rules) and handwashing.
	- Basic welfare area (tool storage, break spot) within agreed boundaries.
- 1.6.4 Prepare simple on-site rules sheet
	- Clean-up expectations, noise limits, smoking rules, key handling.

# 2. Demolition & Structural Works

Demolition and structural work covers safe removal of existing finishes and
constructions, and opening access to hidden services (wires, pipes, ducts) so
they can be corrected or rerouted.

## 2.1 Pre-Demolition Checks

- 2.1.1 Identify walls, ceilings and floors to open
	- List locations with wrong wiring, closed junction boxes, or blocked service routes.
- 2.1.2 Verify structural and building limits
	- Check which walls are structural, allowed chase depths, and slab drilling rules.
- 2.1.3 Locate critical utilities before opening
	- Double-check for hidden pipes, gas lines, and cables to avoid accidental damage.

## 2.2 Opening for Wires, Pipes & HVAC

- 2.2.1 Open access to existing wrong or unsafe wiring
	- Carefully remove plaster/finishes to expose junctions, splices and cable routes.
- 2.2.2 Open routes for new electrical conduits
	- Create channels or openings where future power and low-voltage conduits will run.
- 2.2.3 Open routes for water and waste pipes
	- Floor/wall openings for planned plumbing routes (bathrooms, kitchen, boiler).
- 2.2.4 Open routes and penetrations for HVAC/ventilation
	- Open ceilings, shafts or wall penetrations for ducts and AC lines (towards balcony).
- 2.2.5 Remove or modify blocking elements
	- Remove small non-structural infills that block access to meters, valves or cables.

## 2.3 General Demolition

- 2.3.1 Remove unwanted finishes
	- Old tiles, flooring, ceiling cladding, wall finishes as per design.
- 2.3.2 Remove non-structural partitions (if any)
	- Demolish light partitions marked for removal; protect remaining structure.
- 2.3.3 Remove old fixtures and equipment
	- Old radiators, sanitary ware, kitchen cabinets, old AC units as planned.

## 2.4 Making Good Temporary Edges

- 2.4.1 Stabilize exposed edges and openings
	- Smooth sharp edges, temporary supports if needed around larger openings.
- 2.4.2 Clean and prepare opened areas for MEP work
	- Clear debris so electricians/plumbers/HVAC can work safely and accurately.

# 3. MEP Rough-In (Mechanical, Electrical, Plumbing)

MEP rough-in is all hidden work before finishes: pipes, drains, cables, boxes and
mounting frames inside walls, floors and ceilings. Goal: everything tested and
ready before closing walls.

## 3.1 Electrical Rough-In (Power)

- 3.1.1 Confirm final electrical power layout
	- Outlet, switch, light point locations by room; special circuits (oven, hob, AC).
	- Include balcony/outdoor sockets, lights and any heater or grill connection.
- 3.1.2 Mark routes and box locations on site
	- Mark chases, ceiling routes, junction box and switch/outlet box positions.
	- Clearly mark balcony/outdoor boxes and penetration points through exterior wall.
- 3.1.3 Create wall and ceiling chases (if needed)
	- Cut/chisel channels for conduits; respect structural and building limits.
- 3.1.4 Install conduits, boxes and cable routes (power)
	- Conduits in walls/ceilings, secure boxes at correct depth and orientation.
- 3.1.5 Pull cables for power and lighting
	- Label circuits; keep separation from low-voltage/data routes.
- 3.1.6 Test continuity and insulation of power circuits
	- Basic tests before closing walls; record which circuits are complete.

## 3.2 Low-Voltage & Data Rough-In

- 3.2.1 Confirm low-voltage and data layout
	- Internet/router location, Wi‑Fi access points, security cameras, intercom, TV.
- 3.2.2 Mark low-voltage routes and box locations
	- Wall/ceiling routes and boxes for data, PoE and control cables.
- 3.2.3 Install conduits and boxes for low-voltage
	- Separate conduits or cable paths from power circuits; avoid interference.
- 3.2.4 Pull data and PoE cables
	- Internet/data cables from entry point/router to rooms; TV and intercom lines.
	- PoE-capable runs for router (if remote), access points and security camera points.
- 3.2.5 Rough-in control and weak-current cabling
	- Cables for sensors, smart devices, thermostats and other low-current systems.
- 3.2.6 Test continuity of data/low-voltage lines
	- Basic checks for each run before closing walls.

## 3.3 Plumbing Rough-In (Water & Waste)

- 3.2.1 Confirm plumbing layout for water & waste
	- Fixture locations (WC, basin, shower, kitchen sink, dishwasher, washing machine).
- 3.2.2 Shut off and secure existing water lines
	- Temporary caps/valves; coordinate shutdown times with building rules.
- 3.2.3 Route and fix new water supply lines
	- Cold/hot water piping routes in walls/floors; slopes, insulation where needed.
- 3.2.4 Route and fix new waste/vent lines
	- Drains from fixtures with proper slope and access; check noise/stack rules.
- 3.2.5 Install in-wall frames and mounting kits (if any)
	- WC frames, concealed mixers, shower sets, niche supports.
- 3.2.6 Pressure test water lines
	- Perform pressure test before closing; document results.
- 3.2.7 Leak and flow check for drains
	- Basic water test for new drains and connections.

## 3.4 Gas Rough-In

- 3.3.1 Confirm gas layout and appliances
	- Gas hob, boiler, other gas appliances; allowed locations and ventilation needs.
- 3.3.2 Confirm gas meter location and access
	- Existing or new meter position, reading access, and shut-off valve location.
- 3.3.3 Shut off and secure existing gas lines
	- Temporary caps/valves; coordinate shutdown with building/utility provider.
- 3.3.4 Route and fix new gas lines
	- Gas piping routes, shut-off valve locations, appliance connection points per code.
- 3.3.5 Provide ventilation and combustion air paths
	- Vent openings, ducts or windows as required by code and appliance manuals.
- 3.3.6 Perform gas tightness / pressure test (by certified person)
	- Official leak/pressure test and documentation before closing.
- 3.3.7 Book official inspection / sign-off (where required)
	- Coordinate with certified contractor/authority for final approval of rough-in.

## 3.5 Mechanical / HVAC Prep
- 3.4.1 Confirm HVAC / ventilation concept
	- Locations for indoor units, ducting, grilles, and exhaust/intake points.
- 3.4.2 Provide openings and routes for ducts/pipes
	- Wall/ceiling openings, sleeves through partitions, routes to balconies/shafts.
- 3.4.3 Install supports and brackets
	- Mounting supports for indoor units, duct hangers, vibration isolation where needed.
- 3.4.4 Rough-in condensate and drain lines
	- Ensure gravity slope or plan condensate pumps; avoid future leak risks.
- 3.4.5 Pull control and power cables for HVAC
	- Cables for indoor units, thermostats, control panels.
- 3.4.6 Basic functional checks (where possible)
	- Verify routing, clearances and access before closing walls/ceilings.

# 4. HVAC (Heating, Ventilation, Air Conditioning)

This section covers installation and commissioning of the heating system (gas boiler,
heated floors, radiators), domestic hot water distribution, and air conditioning.

## 4.1 Heating System: Gas Boiler & Distribution

- 4.1.1 Confirm heating and hot water design
	- Boiler capacity, heating zones, underfloor vs radiator circuits, and DHW demand.
- 4.1.2 Install / connect gas water boiler
	- Mount boiler, connect to gas, flue, condensate (if required) and power.
- 4.1.3 Install manifolds and piping for heated floors
	- Manifold location, pipe routes in floor, insulation and expansion joints as needed.
- 4.1.4 Install radiators and bedroom heating elements
	- Radiator locations under windows or designated walls; valves and air vents.
- 4.1.5 Pressure test heating circuits
	- Water pressure test for floor loops and radiator circuits before closing floors/walls.
- 4.1.6 Commission heating system
	- Fill, vent, balance circuits; initial boiler start-up by qualified technician.

## 4.2 Domestic Hot Water (DHW)

- 4.2.1 Route hot water to bathrooms and kitchen
	- Supply lines from boiler to two bathrooms and kitchen, with recirculation if needed.
- 4.2.2 Install DHW mixing and safety components
	- Mixing valves, safety valves, filters and check valves as required.
- 4.2.3 Test hot water delivery
	- Check flow, temperature and waiting time at each outlet.

## 4.3 Air Conditioning: Split Units

- 4.3.1 Confirm AC unit locations and capacity
	- Two indoor units (e.g. living room + bedroom) and one outdoor unit on balcony.
- 4.3.2 Mount indoor units and prepare wall penetrations
	- Brackets, condensate outlets, and holes for refrigerant/power lines.
- 4.3.3 Mount outdoor unit on balcony
	- Brackets or platform, vibration pads, and required clearances.
- 4.3.4 Install refrigerant lines, condensate drains and power
	- Copper piping, insulation, cables and drains between indoor units and balcony unit.
- 4.3.5 Vacuum, leak test and charge system (by certified technician)
	- Evacuation, pressure test, charge as per manufacturer specification.
- 4.3.6 Functional test and commissioning
	- Test cooling/heating modes, condensate drainage and controls.

# 6. Drywall, Partitions & Ceilings

This section covers construction of partitions, wall linings and ceiling systems
after MEP rough-in is complete.

## 6.1 Partitions & Wall Linings

- 6.1.1 Confirm partition layout and wall build-ups
	- Thickness, board types (standard, moisture-resistant), and any sound upgrades.
- 6.1.2 Install partition framing
	- Metal/wood studs, tracks and bracing; check plumb and alignment.
- 6.1.3 Install wall linings and boards
	- First and second side boards, cutouts for boxes, pipes and inspection hatches.
- 6.1.4 Treat joints and corners
	- Joint tape, filler and corner beads as per system requirements.

## 6.2 Ceilings

- 6.2.1 Confirm ceiling levels and drops
	- Heights, bulkheads and special zones (around ducts, beams, curtain tracks).
- 6.2.2 Install ceiling framing
	- Main runners, hangers and cross profiles; level check.
- 6.2.3 Board ceilings and create access panels
	- Fix boards, leave openings for lights, vents, access to junctions where needed.
- 6.2.4 Jointing and surface preparation
	- Joint tape, filler and sanding ready for painting.

## 6.3 Coordination With Services

- 6.3.1 Check MEP rough-in before closing
	- Confirm all cables, pipes, ducts and boxes are in place and tested.
- 6.3.2 Protect and label hidden services
	- Photos and notes of routes; labels on access panels and key junctions.

# 13. Doors, Windows & Hardware

This section covers fitting and adjusting all doors and windows, including balcony
and entrance door, plus installing associated hardware.

## 13.1 Interior Doors

- 13.1.1 Confirm interior door schedule and swings
	- Types, finishes, glazing (if any), opening direction and clear widths by room.
- 13.1.2 Check and prepare openings
	- Verify rough opening sizes, plumb and level; adjust openings where necessary.
- 13.1.3 Install door frames and linings
	- Fix frames plumb and square; use packers/foam; protect finished faces.
- 13.1.4 Hang interior door leaves
	- Fit hinges, hang leaves, check clearances to floor and frames.
- 13.1.5 Install interior door hardware
	- Handles, locks, latches, magnetic catches and door stops.
- 13.1.6 Final adjustment of interior doors
	- Adjust for smooth closing, even gaps and correct latch operation.

## 13.2 Windows & Balcony Door

- 13.2.1 Verify window and balcony door types
	- Opening direction, safety glazing where required, and ventilation options.
- 13.2.2 Level and align frames
	- Check plumb, level and squareness; pack and fix to maintain alignment.
- 13.2.3 Seal around frames
	- Use appropriate foam/tape/sealant inside and outside, respecting vapor barrier logic.
- 13.2.4 Install and adjust sashes/leaf
	- Ensure smooth operation, correct compression on seals and secure locking.

## 13.3 Entrance Door

- 13.3.1 Check entrance door frame and level
	- Verify frame plumb/level, threshold height and clear closing.
- 13.3.2 Modify inner face to match design
	- Prepare and clad/paint the inner side of the entrance door to match interior design.
- 13.3.3 Install or replace hardware and seals
	- Lock, cylinder, handles, peephole, seals and threshold as required.
- 13.3.4 Security and operation check
	- Confirm smooth closing, multi-point locking (if any) and key operation.

## 13.4 General Hardware & Accessories

- 13.4.1 Install door stops and closers (if needed)
	- Prevent handle-to-wall impacts and control closing where required.
- 13.4.2 Install window handles, restrictors and stays
	- Child safety restrictors or ventilation stays if part of design.
